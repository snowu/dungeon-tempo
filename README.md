# Dungeon Tempo

**Play:** https://snowu.github.io/dungeon-tempo/

A rhythm roguelike about a dungeon band. Hit falling gems with A/S/D/F (or arrows, or D/F/J/K — switchable above the lanes) or touch to damage enemies. Only notes that pass the line cost health; early, late and wrong-key taps are free and explained on screen. First-time players start with an eight-note practice that never costs health (remembered in `localStorage` as `tempo-learned`). Beginner (default) runs at 72 BPM with 16 health, weaker enemies and notes two beats apart in the first two rooms. A run has five procedurally generated encounters, with a boss at the end. After each victory, choose one of three relics, then choose the standard crypt or a faster elite chamber. Elite wins heal 2 health. Relics modify damage, healing, maximum health, hit windows and shielding. Death resets the run; only your best score persists locally.

**You are the band.** Each lane is a bandmate playing one part of the soundtrack: knight = drums (kick and snare), mage = melody, rogue = bass, skeleton = chords. Charts are generated from the arrangement itself (`roomChart` in `dist/expedition.js` reads `compose()`), so every gem is a real note. Each bar spotlights one bandmate, and the first bar is always drums. Missing a note drops that part out of the mix (`Music.setLane`) and greys out the bandmate until you hit their lane again. Lane bus levels per soundtrack keep every part audible against the drums.

Each room has a four-beat count-in and a 36-beat time limit. Defeat the enemy before the music stops. Runs are generated from a random seed, shown beside the inventory. Patterns, enemies and relic offers are deterministic for that seed and route. Three selectable original soundtracks: Neon Catacombs (synthwave), Graveyard Shift (dungeon funk), and 8-Bit Afterlife (chiptune). Preview any track before a run, or switch between rooms. Each has an eight-bar arrangement with separate percussion, bass, chords and a lead melody. Tempo follows the room; later rooms add percussion. Hit sounds follow the current chord. Audio uses the Web Audio clock; pausing suspends that clock, and hiding the tab auto-pauses. Timing offset accommodates audio latency: set it by hand, or press ◎ Calibrate (above the lanes or in the sidebar) and tap along to 12 audio-only clicks at 90 BPM. The median tap error becomes the offset (clamped to ±200 ms) and is saved in `localStorage` as `tempo-offset`.

Run `npm run dev`, then open http://localhost:8087. Pushing to `main` runs `npm test` and deploys `dist/` to GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`). Run timing tests with `npm test`. Deployable files are in `dist/`.

Art: **Kenney — Tiny Dungeon**, CC0, https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon. Original license is included at `dist/assets/Kenney-License.txt`. The packed sprite sheet was obtained unchanged from the public mirror https://github.com/chaewonkong/tiny_dungeon/tree/main/assets/kenney_tiny-dungeon after the original download failed. Sprites were inspected before selecting the characters and wall tiles. Music is composed and synthesized in `dist/music.js`, with no external recordings. Fonts are optional Google Fonts with system fallbacks.

Verification: `npm test` covers key layouts, practice chart, tap explanations, beginner tuning, seeded generation, route tradeoffs, relic effects, damage, permadeath, a full five-room victory, UI reward/route transitions, scoring, pause and restart. Browser integration test: `node tests/browser.cjs` with a running local server and Playwright installed (both browser tests skip practice and select Normal + D/F/J/K) (or `PLAYWRIGHT_PATH`; optional `CHROME_PATH`). It plays all five rooms using real audio-clock timing, mixes keyboard and touch input, chooses an elite route, collects relics, defeats the boss, then verifies death and restart. Desktop and mobile screenshots are written to `/tmp/rogue-*.png`. `node tests/audio-browser.cjs` renders all three arrangements, checks mix headroom, previews each track, verifies gameplay audio controls, and checks that the keycaps are centred precisely on their lanes at desktop, tablet and mobile sizes. Touch targets are at least 44 pixels. `node tests/calibration-browser.cjs` simulates a player tapping 60 ms late and checks the measured offset, persistence and the too-few-taps path.
